## Runtime Boundary

`volume5_finale_roleplay` and `volume5_epilogue_roleplay` are separate
free-form live-NPC experiences. The selected NPC generates prose from the
current scene, motive, participants, and pinned Knowledge. An independent
evaluator proposes bounded score/evidence changes. Only the deterministic
Roleplay and Campaign state machines may advance nodes or select endings.

Provider failure leaves the turn uncommitted. Structural attacks skip model
calls and cannot alter scores, evidence, relationships, skills, letters, or
routes.

1. `yunyun_recognition_without_label_pressure`: acknowledge Yunyun's observed
   contribution without forcing a nickname, invitation, or public performance.
2. `classmate_reconnection_and_self_named_friendship`: let every person choose
   how to describe the renewed friendship and reject ridicule as proof of
   closeness.
3. `public_relationship_claim_and_privacy_boundary`: separate teasing or a
   boast from a current relationship agreement and keep private history private.
4. `excellent_mage_question_clarified`: clarify whether "an excellent mage"

   the answer as a decision.

   advanced-magic diversification, and deferral without converting coercion,
   abduction, or counterfactual self-blame into evidence of incompetence.

   require her explicit current allocation, and prohibit proxy operation,
   prophecy, debt, or party convenience from choosing a skill.

   establish an abort signal, then record Megumin's own selected or deferred
   path without rewriting it after the effect is observed.

## Shared Scores

- `fact_and_provenance`: observed action, source, claims, ownership, and
  uncertainty remain distinct.
- `social_agency`: names, invitations, friendships, privacy, skill choices,
  letters, and participation remain current and voluntary.
- `capability_planning`: the party compares real capabilities, constraints,
  alternatives, recovery, and role assignments.
- `responsible_power`: no coercive card operation, dangerous spell, Drain
  Touch, restraint, theft, or royal commitment bypasses authority and safety.

## Campaign Contract

All Chapter 5 endings route to the Finale. Every Finale ending routes to the
Epilogue while sealing its own scores, evidence, and relationships. The three
Epilogue endings complete the seven-entry Volume 5 Campaign without importing
Volume 6 facts.
