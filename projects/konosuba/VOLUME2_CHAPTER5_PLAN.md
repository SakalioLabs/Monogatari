# Volume 2 Chapter 5 Dynamic Roleplay Plan

This chapter adapts the Destroyer emergency as a scene-bound, real-time NPC
roleplay. Source chronology and public situation facts come from the user's
local EPUB. Runtime NPC replies, evaluation language, recovery text, and route
outcomes are newly authored or generated.

## Core interaction contract

- The player writes free-form commands and negotiations as Kazuma.
- Aqua, Darkness, Megumin, and Wiz answer independently from their character,
  active scene, pinned Knowledge, relationship, and bounded transcript.
- The NPC model produces only the visible in-character reply.
- A separate evaluator proposes clamped score, evidence, and active-speaker
  relationship changes.
- The deterministic Roleplay state machine alone advances phases and selects an
  ending. Neither model may declare a barrier, leg group, core, heat state, or
  teleport destination resolved without the authored evidence gate.
- Fixed Dialogue is limited to bounded ending epilogues and inference recovery;
  it is not a playable conversation path.

## Planned live nodes

1. Establish evacuation, frontline, casting, medical, and retreat zones.
2. Test Aqua's barrier break with an observable success or abort condition.
3. Coordinate Megumin and Wiz against opposite leg groups in one action window.
4. Hold the civilian boundary while reporting injury and retreat capacity.
5. Confirm that stopped movement does not prove the fortress safe.
6. Bound the boarding team, golem response, retreat signal, and head count.
7. Separate notebook claims from observed core and heat conditions.
8. Remove the coronatite core only after explicit, revocable magic-transfer
   consent and record the transfer separately from the core action.
9. Compare known and random teleport risk without treating luck as evidence.
10. Vent stored heat through a separately consented transfer and final
    overcharged Explosion, then close the civilian and casualty ledger.

## Score dimensions

- `phased_command`: explicit phase ownership, signals, handoffs, and state checks.
- `civilian_evacuation`: evacuation, injury rotation, retreat, and casualty care.
- `hazard_verification`: barrier, mobility, core, stored heat, and destination
  claims grounded in current evidence.
- `consensual_magic_transfer`: participant-specific scope, limits, revocation,
  and separate confirmation of transfer and use.

Scores are route inputs, not permission to invent facts. Every phase-changing
condition also requires its named evidence record.

## Planned endings

- `volume2_destroyer_defeated`: all command, evacuation, hazard, consent, and
  final heat-release evidence is complete.
- `volume2_axel_survives_at_cost`: the immediate threat ends, but incomplete
  phase or responsibility evidence leaves preventable harm or unresolved risk.
- `volume2_destroyer_heat_disaster`: safe turns expire without a verified heat
  response and complete civilian boundary.

## Required verification

- Provider-free preview visits every node and reaches all three endings through
  authored conditions.
- Quality replay covers every score and evidence boundary plus multilingual,
  structural, memory, score, evidence, tool, and ending forgery.
- Every adversarial turn is guarded with zero score, evidence, relationship, or
  phase mutation.
- Browser Playtest proves free-form input, selectable live NPCs, independent
  generation/evaluation, deterministic transition, and diegetic fallback.
- Forced NPC and evaluator failures, including ORT allocation failure, commit a
  degraded in-scene recovery instead of exposing technical error text.
