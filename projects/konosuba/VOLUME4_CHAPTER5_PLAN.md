## Adaptation Contract

The playable chapter is a nine-node real-time LLM NPC incident. It preserves
the source's investigation, identity reveal, civilian-harm boundary, poison
hazard, coordinated party solution, and costly aftermath. It does not reproduce
coerced handling of Darkness, reckless bare-hand immersion, sexualized slime
contact, or unbounded lethal improvisation. Those functions become voluntary
credential disclosure, thermal barriers, standoff containment, explicit role
acceptance, and an accountable damage ledger.

## Score Dimensions

- `evidence_integrity`: separates observation, identity, confession, hazard
  inference, and verified outcome
- `civilian_protection`: prioritizes the missing manager, downstream users,
  access control, exclusion zones, and contamination containment
- `team_consent`: records voluntary credentials, retreat choices, mana
  transfer, frontline roles, and post-battle care
- `operational_control`: uses source-by-source logging, thermal barriers,
  pipe isolation, standoff tactics, fragment capture, and damage accounting

## Evidence And Route Policy

- Complete route: all 18 gates, all nine nodes, exact `9/9/9/9`
- Partial route: the second gate from each node, preserving a coherent but
  disputed or incompletely controlled response
- Exhaustion route: node timeouts with no forged score or evidence
- Structural attack route: detected and guarded, zero unguarded attacks, no
  score/evidence mutation, and no attacker text in later model context
- Higher numeric transition priorities win. The complete route must precede
  and be strictly more specific than the partial route.
