# Volume 6 Chapter 4 Roleplay Plan

`volume6_chapter4_roleplay` is a nine-node real-time interaction graph, not a
fixed Dialogue.

## Source and adaptation boundary

The chapter design was grounded in all 633 heading and paragraph nodes across
nine sections of the user's local Volume 6 EPUB,
`OEBPS/Text/chapter04.xhtml`. It preserves the mobilization, death and revival,
artifact discovery, body swap, return, banquet, and final threat chronology
without copying source prose.

The source's body-swap privacy violations are not forced player actions.
The adaptation converts them into explicit bodily-autonomy, disclosure,
repair, supervision, and recovery decisions. Runtime NPC wording is generated
from the active scene, selected character, node-local motive, bounded
transcript, relationship, and pinned Knowledge.

## Runtime loop

1. The party establishes a bounded royal-capital mobilization plan.
2. Eris separates current death, revival, and two missing-artifact facts.
3. The battlefield aftermath records contribution and care without exaggeration.
4. The royal artifact is identified before anyone experiments with it.
5. The accidental body swap triggers immediate reciprocal body boundaries.
6. Iris may explore only through explicit safeguards and stop conditions.
7. The returned pair discloses impact and defines concrete repair.
8. The banquet records contribution and party belonging without proxy speech.
9. Chris's mortality warning becomes a supervised artifact-recovery decision.

The NPC model generates only in-scene prose. A separate evaluator may propose
clamped score deltas and exact player-quote evidence. Only the deterministic
Roleplay state machine advances nodes or selects an ending.

## Route authority

Four scores govern the route: `battlefield_discipline`, `bodily_autonomy`,
`truth_and_provenance`, and `party_belonging`. Eighteen independent evidence
gates prevent a model from converting confidence, royal status, accidental
access, silence, embarrassment, or claimed danger into consent or route
authority.

The reachable endings are:

- `volume6_joint_artifact_recovery_with_consent`
- `volume6_guarded_artifact_recovery_plan`
- `volume6_body_swap_risk_unresolved`

Fixed Dialogue is limited to the three short post-ending epilogues.
